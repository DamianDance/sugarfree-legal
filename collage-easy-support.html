Free editing

Smart Layout and the core collage editor are available without a subscription. You can arrange photos, adjust the grid, add text and stickers, apply standard filters, and export a social-ready image.

AI tools and Premium

AI image editing, background tools, object erasing and AI sticker generation require either an active Pro subscription or purchased AI credits. Credits can be purchased and used without starting a subscription. Available products and prices are shown in the app before purchase.

Common help

  • Use Restore Purchases in Settings if an active purchase is not detected.
  • Check Photos permission in System Settings if saving does not complete.
  • AI tools require an internet connection and enough available credits or Pro access.
  • Include your device model, operating-system version and app version when reporting a problem.
