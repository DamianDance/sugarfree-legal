Collage Easy Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Overview

Collage Easy is a photo collage and photo editing app. Most editing features run locally on your device. This policy explains what data may be processed when you use the app, AI image editing, subscriptions, analytics, export and sharing features.

Photos and user content

Photos you choose from your library are used to create and edit your collage. Standard editing tools such as layouts, filters, text, stickers, frame colors and export are processed on your device. Your photos, written overlay text and sticker content are not sent to Mixpanel.

AI image editing

If you use AI image editing, the selected image and your prompt are sent to the configured AI processing service so the requested edit can be generated. The app may include request metadata such as app version, request ID, installation ID, input size, selected model policy and usage-limit information to operate the service, prevent abuse and control costs.

Analytics

If you allow analytics, Collage Easy sends anonymous product usage events to Mixpanel using the EU endpoint. These events help understand feature usage, onboarding, editor actions, AI edit outcomes, paywall flow, exports, shares and saves. Photos, prompt text, written overlay text and image payloads are not sent to Mixpanel. You can change analytics consent in the app settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscription purchase status is handled by Apple through the App Store and by RevenueCat for entitlement management. Collage Easy does not receive your payment card details. Apple manages subscription renewals, cancellations and refunds through your Apple ID.

Local data

The app may store local preferences on your device, including onboarding state, editing limits, analytics consent, subscription entitlement state, selected editor settings and cached creative state needed for the app to function.

Sharing and export

When you export, save or share a collage, the generated image is passed to iOS sharing or Photos features at your request. The destination service you choose may process the image according to its own privacy practices.

Tracking and advertising

Collage Easy does not use third-party advertising and does not use analytics events for cross-app tracking or advertising data broker purposes.
